Depot Test Equipment

We design, build and supply component test equipment used in the periodic maintenance of rolling stock.

Inverter & C/I Tester

Verifies VVVF inverter and converter units that control traction motors.

Power, start/restart, protection circuit, gate signal and main circuit tests

Static Inverter (SIV) Tester

Verifies SIV controllers that supply power to cabin lighting and HVAC.

Power, start/restart, protection circuit and gate output tests

Circuit Breaker Tester

Tests line breaker boxes and high-speed circuit breakers.

HSCB, LB sequence, DCCT/DCPT, insulation and withstand voltage tests

Traction Motor Tester

Tests traction motors, with 1, 4 or 8 test benches.

Forward/reverse rotation, high-speed and insulation tests

Brake System Tester

Tests brake operating units (BOU) and brake electronic control units (ECU).

BOU, ECU, pressure sensor and insulation tests

Brake Valve Tester

Tests individual valves of the brake system.

Valve function and insulation tests

Brake Cylinder Tester

Tests the performance of brake cylinders.

Air leakage, pressure, slack adjuster, output and release tests

Block Brake Tester

Tests the performance of tread brake units.

Air leakage, pressure, slack adjustment, output and release tests

Air Compressor Tester

Tests main and auxiliary air compressors.

Air leakage, performance, safety valve and insulation tests

Oil Damper Tester (Hydraulic)

Tests oil dampers using a hydraulic unit.

Damping force measurement

Oil Damper Tester (Servo Motor)

Tests oil dampers using a servo motor drive.

Damping force measurement

Air Spring Tester

Tests suspension air springs.

Air tightness and pressure tests

Pantograph Tester

Tests the performance of pantographs.

Rise/fall time, contact force, minimum pressure, air leakage and max height tests

Master Controller Tester

Tests one-handle and two-handle master controllers in the driver's cab.

Dead man switch, activation key, handles, potentiometer output and insulation tests

Relay Tester

Tests control relays of high-speed trains and EMUs.

Pick-up/drop-out voltage and time, coil and contact resistance, chattering tests

Jumper Coupler Tester

Tests inter-car jumper couplers.

Continuity, vibration and insulation tests

HVAC Controller Tester

Tests railway HVAC controllers.

Voltage variation, sensor, TCMS communication, power detection and mode switch tests

ATC Tester

Tests on-board ATC equipment.

Driving mode, speed detection, I/O, status and power tests

ATC/ATS Tester

Tests combined ATC/ATS on-board equipment.

I/O, changeover, overspeed, simulated running, fault detection and signal response tests

TCMS Tester

Tests train control and monitoring systems (TCMS).

DI, DO, communication, power and insulation tests

PA & Display Tester

Tests public address and passenger information displays.

Voltage variation, electrical characteristics, automatic/manual PA and display tests

Axle Ultrasonic Testing System

Polishes axles and inspects them by ultrasonic testing.

Axle polishing; 0°, 17°, 24°, 45° and 55° probes

Magnetic Particle Inspection System

Detects surface defects in couplers and fittings by magnetic particle inspection.

Magnetic particle inspection

High-Speed Train Gearbox Tester

Tests motor gearboxes of high-speed trains.

Rotation, temperature and vibration tests

Drive Gear No-Load Tester

Tests EMU drive gear assemblies under no load.

No-load running, temperature, lubricant temperature and vibration tests

Partner Equipment (EASYDUR)

We supply test equipment from our partner EASYDUR.

Oil damper, bogie, spring, hardness (world's largest capacity), vision and wheel testers

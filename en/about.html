CEO Message

Thank you for visiting NSTek.

We put creating value for our customers first. Through continuous growth and technology development, we strive to increase our corporate value and contribute to our customers and society.

Since our founding in 2006, we have focused on rolling stock parts, test equipment and inspection systems. Through steady research, development and marketing, we work to become the most trusted company in the railway industry.

Everyone at NSTek is committed to repaying our customers with outstanding quality and service, and to growing steadily as a company that earns your trust.

Park Hyun-soo, CEO
